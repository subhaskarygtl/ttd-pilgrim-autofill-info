Features

Built for the rush of a TTD slot release

Everything is aimed at one moment: the pilgrim details page, when every second counts.

3 pilgrims in ~3 seconds

Names, age, gender and ID details go into every row at once instead of field by field.

Up to 6 pilgrims

Save your whole family or group once and fill every pilgrim in a single booking.

Aadhaar & passport + visa

Works with Aadhaar, and with passport and visa details for NRI and foreign pilgrims.

Aadhaar typo check

Flags an Aadhaar number that looks mistyped before it reaches the booking form.

Duplicate ID check

Warns you if the same ID number has been entered for two different pilgrims.

Keyboard shortcuts

Alt+A fills the form. Alt+S fills and moves you on to the next step.

Floating button

Prefer the mouse? A small floating button on the page fills everything with one click.

Dark mode

Easy on the eyes when you are waiting up for a late-night or early-morning slot release.

How it works

Four simple steps

Set it up once, then use it every time you book.

Get access & install

Request access by email. Once approved, add the extension to Google Chrome on your computer.

Save your pilgrims

Enter each pilgrim's name, age, gender and ID proof once. The typo and duplicate checks run here.

Book on TTD as usual

Log in to the TTD website yourself, wait your turn in the queue and choose your darshan or seva slot.

Press Alt+A

On the pilgrim details page, press the shortcut or tap the floating button. Check the details, then continue and pay yourself.

Safety & fair use

It saves typing. It does not cheat.

The extension only fills the pilgrim details form. Everything that is security-checked or involves money stays with you.

No login automation

You sign in to TTD yourself. The extension never asks for or stores your TTD password.

No OTP automation

OTPs are read and typed by you. The extension does not touch your phone or messages.

No CAPTCHA solving

CAPTCHAs are there to prove a person is booking. You solve them — always.

No payment automation

You review the booking and complete the payment yourself. No card or UPI details are stored.

Follows the TTD queue

It does not skip or bypass TTD's waiting room. You wait your turn like every other devotee.

Your data stays in your Chrome

Pilgrim details are saved locally in your own Chrome browser — not uploaded anywhere.

Please note: this is an independent tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams (TTD). Always follow TTD's booking rules, and check every pilgrim's details before you confirm a booking.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is this an official TTD app?

No. TTD Pilgrim Autofill is an independent tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams (TTD). Bookings are always made on the official TTD website.

Will it book darshan tickets for me automatically?

No. It only fills the pilgrim details form. You log in, wait in the queue, pick the slot, enter the OTP, solve any CAPTCHA and pay — yourself.

Which bookings does it help with?

It helps on the pilgrim details step of TTD online bookings such as Special Entry Darshan and Arjitha Seva, where the details of every pilgrim have to be entered quickly.

How many pilgrims can I fill?

Up to 6 pilgrims per booking. Three pilgrims fill in about 3 seconds.

Which ID proofs are supported?

Aadhaar, and passport with visa details for NRI and foreign pilgrims. Aadhaar numbers get a typo check, and every pilgrim is checked for duplicate IDs.

Where is my pilgrims' data stored?

Only in your own Chrome browser on your computer. It is not uploaded to any server.

Does it work on mobile phones?

No. Chrome extensions do not run in Chrome on Android or iPhone. Use Google Chrome on a Windows, Mac, Linux or ChromeOS computer.

How do I get it?

The extension is shared privately. Request access by email and you will receive the setup steps.
